# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## 1. Business Objectives
The primary business objective is to develop a mobile application that helps individuals manage their personal expenses effectively, thereby increasing financial literacy and reducing unnecessary spending.

## 2. Stakeholder Analysis
| Stakeholder | Role | Interest | Influence | Key Concerns |
|------------|------|----------|-----------|-------------|
| Product Owner | Project Manager | High | High | Ensuring the product meets market needs and business goals. |
| Development Team | Engineers | High | Medium | Meeting technical requirements and delivering a high-quality application. |
| Marketing Team | Strategist | Medium | High | Promoting the app to target users and driving adoption. |
| Legal Team | Compliance Officer | Low | High | Ensuring compliance with data privacy and payment regulations. |
| Users | Individual Financiers | High | High | Ease of use, accuracy, and value for money. |

## 3. RACI Matrix
| Activity | Responsible | Accountable | Consulted | Informed |
|----------|------------|-------------|-----------|----------|
| Requirement gathering | Product Owner | Product Owner | Development Team | Development Team |
| Design & development | Development Team | Development Team | Product Owner, Marketing Team | Product Owner, Marketing Team |
| Testing & QA | Development Team | Development Team | Product Owner | Product Owner |
| Launch & promotion | Marketing Team | Marketing Team | Product Owner, Legal Team | Product Owner, Legal Team |

## 4. Market Analysis
- **Target market size:** Estimated to be around 10 million individuals in the U.S. and Europe.
- **Competitor landscape:**
  - Mint.com
  - Personal Capital
  - YNAB (You Need A Budget)
  - Expensify
  - Quicken
- **Market positioning:** Our app offers a user-friendly, AI-driven solution that simplifies expense tracking and budgeting.

## 5. Revenue Model
- **Pricing strategy:** Freemium model with premium features.
- **Projected pricing tiers:**
  - Basic Tier: Free (Basic transaction recording, categorization)
  - Premium Tier: $9.99/month (Advanced analytics, AI-driven predictions, advanced budgeting tools)
- **Revenue projections (Year 1):** $500k
- **Revenue projections (Year 2):** $1 million

## 6. Cost Estimates
| Category | Estimated Cost | Frequency |
|----------|---------------|-----------|
| Infrastructure | $50,000 | One-time |
| Third-party services | $30,000/year | Annual |
| Development | $200,000 | One-time |
| Marketing & Promotion | $100,000 | One-time |

## 7. ROI Projections
- **Initial investment:** $480,000
- **Expected revenue in Year 1:** $500k
- **Break-even period:** Approximately 6 months
- **ROI timeline:** Expected to achieve a positive ROI within the first year.

## 8. Compliance & Legal Requirements
- **Data privacy:** GDPR (for EU users), CCPA (for California residents)
- **Payment compliance:** PCI DSS for any payment processing features.
- **Industry-specific regulations:** None identified at this time, but will be monitored and updated as needed.

## 9. Risk Assessment
| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|-----------|
| User adoption: Low initial user base | High | Medium | Launch targeted marketing campaigns and offer a free trial period. |
| Technical issues: App crashes or bugs | Medium | High | Implement robust testing and continuous integration/continuous deployment (CI/CD) practices. |
| Data breaches: Security vulnerabilities | Low | High | Regularly update security protocols and conduct penetration testing. |

## 10. Timeline & Milestones
| Milestone | Target Date | Deliverables |
|-----------|------------|-------------|
| Requirement gathering & analysis | Q1 | Detailed BRD, user personas, feature list |
| Design & prototyping | Q2 | UI/UX designs, prototype |
| Development | Q3 | Basic functionality (record income/spending, categorize transactions) |
| Testing & QA | Q4 | Bug fixes, usability testing |
| Launch preparation | Q1 (Year 2) | Marketing materials, app store optimization |
| App launch | Q2 (Year 2) | Official release, initial user acquisition |

This BRD outlines the key aspects of developing a mobile application to manage personal expenses effectively, focusing on revenue generation and compliance requirements.
