The Story Behind the Komorebi Form

auto_stories Description & Story expand_more

Inspired by the Japanese concept of komorebi—the interplay of light and leaves as sunlight filters through pine canopies—this vessel explores disciplined symmetry and tactile earthiness.

Each vase is hand-thrown on a Shimpo whisper wheel using coarse buff stoneware sourced from the Pacific Northwest basin. While the clay is leather-hard, twelve vertical flutes are carved freehand with bamboo loop tools, creating delicate shadows that transform throughout the day as indoor light shifts.

Finished with a custom wood-ash and feldspar glaze, fired in high reduction at 1280°C (Cone 10). The glaze pools along the fluted ridges, creating variegated moss-green halos over a warm toasted sand surface.
