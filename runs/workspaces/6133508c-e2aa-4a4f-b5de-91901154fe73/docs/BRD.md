# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## 1. Business Objectives
To develop a premium AI-powered digital closet and smart stylist application named 'Style OS' that offers users a space-themed dark mode aesthetic with advanced features such as AI outfit recommendations and virtual try-on capabilities, thereby increasing user engagement, retention, and revenue.

## 2. Stakeholder Analysis

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest | Influence | Key Concerns |
|------------|------|----------|-----------|-------------|
| Product Manager | Leadership | Functionality, User Experience | High | Ensuring the app meets market needs and user expectations. |
| Marketing Team | Growth | Branding, Launch Strategy | High | Aligning marketing efforts with the product's features to drive adoption. |
| Development Team | Execution | Technical Feasibility, Performance | High | Meeting technical requirements within budget and timeline. |
| Legal Team | Compliance | Data Privacy, Payment Security | High | Ensuring compliance with relevant regulations to protect user data and transactions. |
| Sales Team | Revenue | Pricing Strategy, Upselling Opportunities | Medium | Maximizing revenue through effective pricing and sales strategies. |

## 4. Market Analysis

- **Target Market Size:** Estimated at 10 million users globally by the end of Year 2.
- **Competitor Landscape:**
  - Stitch Fix
  - ASOS
  - ModCloth
  - Zara
  - The North Face
- **Market Positioning:** 'Style OS' will differentiate itself through its advanced AI capabilities, premium space-themed dark mode, and interactive virtual try-on features.

## 5. Revenue Model

- **Pricing Strategy:** Freemium model with subscription tiers.
  - Free Tier: Basic outfit recommendations and weather widget (1 month trial)
  - Premium Tier: All features including virtual try-on and advanced analytics ($9.99/month)
- **Projected Pricing Tiers with Feature Breakdown:**
  - **Free Tier:**
    - Local weather widget
    - 'Style Score' ring chart
    - Basic AI outfit recommendations
  - **Premium Tier:**
    - All features of the Free Tier
    - Interactive virtual try-on screen
    - Closet Health analytics hub
- **Revenue Projections (Year 1, Year 2):**
  - Year 1: $500k in revenue from Premium Tiers
  - Year 2: $1.5M in revenue from Premium Tiers

## 7. ROI Projections

- **Expected Return on Investment Timeline:** 2 years
- **Calculations:**
  - Total Initial Costs: $380,000
  - Annual Revenue from Premium Tiers: $1M (Year 2)
  - Net Profit in Year 2: $600k
  - ROI: 1.5x

## 8. Compliance & Legal Requirements

- **Data Privacy:** GDPR, CCPA compliance
- **Payment Compliance:** PCI DSS compliance
- **Industry-specific Regulations:** None identified at this time.
