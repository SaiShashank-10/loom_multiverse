description About the Role

As a Junior Frontend Engineer on Stripe’s Developer Dashboard team, you will shape the foundation through which millions of global internet businesses manage their billing, analytics, and infrastructure. Our team crafts high-fidelity, exceptionally reliable web experiences that condense intricate financial mechanics into serene, intuitive workflows.

You will work hand-in-hand with senior systems engineers, product designers, and technical writers to ship accessible, lightning-fast UI components. We value crisp execution, deep curiosity about modern JavaScript runtimes, and a genuine obsession with developer ergonomics.
