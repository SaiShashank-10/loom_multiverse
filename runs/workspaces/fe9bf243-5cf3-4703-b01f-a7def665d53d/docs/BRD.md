# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## 1. Business Objectives
The primary business objective is to provide a user-friendly expense management tool that helps individuals track and control their finances effectively. The goal is to increase customer satisfaction and retention through intuitive features, while also generating revenue through subscription tiers.

## 2. Stakeholder Analysis

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest | Influence | Key Concerns |
|------------|------|----------|-----------|-------------|
| Product Manager | Leadership | Functionality, User Experience | High | Ensuring the tool meets user needs and drives adoption. |
| Marketing Team | Leadership | Brand Awareness, Market Penetration | Medium | Promoting the product effectively to target audiences. |
| Sales Team | Leadership | Revenue Growth | High | Achieving sales targets through effective marketing and customer acquisition. |
| Development Team | Technical | Feature Implementation | High | Delivering high-quality features within timelines. |
| Legal Team | Compliance | Data Privacy, Payment Security | High | Ensuring compliance with relevant regulations to protect user data. |

## 4. Market Analysis

- **Target Market Size:** The global personal finance management market is estimated to be around $10 billion by 2025, with a compound annual growth rate of 9%.
- **Competitor Landscape:**
  - Mint
  - Personal Capital
  - YNAB (You Need A Budget)
  - PocketGuard
  - NerdWallet
  - Differentiation:** Our tool offers AI-driven predictions for future spending and customizable budget categories, providing a more personalized experience.
- **Market Positioning:** We position ourselves as the go-to expense management solution that combines simplicity with advanced features to help individuals take control of their finances.

## 5. Revenue Model

- **Pricing Strategy:** Freemium model
- **Projected Pricing Tiers:**
  - Basic Tier (Free): Unlimited income and spending tracking, basic budgeting tools.
  - Standard Tier ($10/month): Advanced budgeting, transaction categorization, AI-driven predictions.
  - Premium Tier ($25/month): All features of the Standard Tier plus personalized financial advice, advanced reporting.

## 6. Cost Estimates

| Category | Estimated Cost | Frequency |
|----------|---------------|-----------|
| Infrastructure | $10,000 (AWS hosting) | Monthly |
| Third-party services | $5,000 (AI services, analytics tools) | Monthly |
| Development | $20,000 (Initial development) + $10,000/year (Maintenance and updates) | One-time |

## 7. ROI Projections

- **Year 1:** Expected revenue of $50,000 from the Basic Tier, $30,000 from the Standard Tier, and $20,000 from the Premium Tier.
- **Year 2:** Revenue projections increase to $80,000 from the Basic Tier, $45,000 from the Standard Tier, and $30,000 from the Premium Tier.

## 8. Compliance & Legal Requirements

- **Data Privacy:** GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
- **Payment Compliance:** PCI DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard)
- **Industry-specific regulations:** None applicable for a general expense management tool.

## 9. Risk Assessment

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|-----------|
| User Adoption | High | Medium | Conduct thorough market research and offer free trial periods. |
| Technical Issues | Medium | High | Implement robust testing processes and have a dedicated support team. |
| Competition | High | Medium | Continuously improve the product with new features and better user experience. |
